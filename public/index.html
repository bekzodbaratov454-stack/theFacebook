About BlogSpace

A thoughtful place for writers, thinkers, and curious minds to share ideas with the world.

What is BlogSpace?

BlogSpace is an open blogging platform built for simplicity. No distractions, no ads — just writing and reading. Whether you want to share a personal story, a tutorial, or your latest thoughts, BlogSpace gives you a clean space to do it.

Every user can write posts, leave comments, and connect through ideas.

How it works

Create an account — sign up with your name, email, and a username. Your profile is yours to customize with a photo.

Write posts — use the "+ Write" button to publish your thoughts. You can add images to make posts more engaging.

Engage with others — like posts you enjoy, leave comments, and read what others are writing.

Track your reading — your profile keeps a history of every post you've read, with the exact date and time.

Built with

BlogSpace is powered by Node.js and Express on the backend, with MongoDB for data storage. Authentication uses JWT tokens with refresh token support. Files are stored locally via Multer.

Contact

Have questions or feedback? Reach out via the community or open an issue on the project repository.

Community Rules

BlogSpace is built on respect and good faith. These rules keep it that way.

Content rules

  • 1.Write original content. Don't copy and paste others' work without attribution.
  • 2.No hate speech, harassment, or content targeting individuals or groups based on identity.
  • 3.No spam, advertisements, or repetitive promotional content.
  • 4.Do not post misinformation — clearly label opinion and satire.
  • 5.No content that violates others' privacy or shares personal information without consent.

Community behaviour

  • 6.Be respectful in comments. Disagree with ideas, not people.
  • 7.Don't use the platform to harass or target specific users.
  • 8.Report content that violates these rules rather than engaging with it.

Account rules

  • 9.One account per person. Don't create multiple accounts to bypass restrictions.
  • 10.Don't share your account credentials with others.
  • 11.Accounts that violate these rules may be deactivated by admins.

Enforcement

Admins reserve the right to remove content or deactivate accounts that violate these rules. Decisions are made in good faith to keep the community healthy.
